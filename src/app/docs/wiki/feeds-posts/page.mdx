# Feeds & posts

## What it is

The social core. On this single-group instance the **group's Feed tab is the
feed** — opening `/` sends you to the group page, and Feed is one of its tabs.
Posts come in four kinds: a plain **social** post, an **offer**, a **request**,
and a **gratitude** post that thanks a specific person.

Posts can be authored by you or, if you're an admin, **as the group**.

<Callout type="note" title="Where the feed is">
  <code>/</code> issues a 307 to <code>/groups/&lt;PRIMARY_AGENT_ID&gt;</code>.
  The <code>(main)/page.tsx</code> file is only a fallback for requests the
  middleware matcher misses — it renders no feed of its own. Individual posts open
  at <code>/posts/[id]</code>.
</Callout>

## How to write a post

1. Go to **`/create`** and stay on the **Post** tab (the composer is also
   embedded on feed surfaces).
2. Click into the composer to expand it.
3. Choose the **post type**:
   - **Social** — an ordinary post. Optionally mark it a **live invitation**
     with a location.
   - **Offer** — attach an existing offering, or create a new one inline. The
     post carries the listing.
   - **Request** — ask for something. (Stock needs post as Requests too — see
     [Projects & jobs](/docs/wiki/projects-jobs#stock-inventory-and-needs-lists).)
   - **Gratitude** — pick a recipient and thank them.
4. Write the body. **URLs in the body are auto-extracted into rich link
   previews.**
5. Set **visibility**. If global visibility is off you must choose at least one
   locale, group, or person — the composer refuses an empty scope.
6. Optionally attach the ecosocial pickers (EFT / capital / audit values) shown
   alongside the composer on `/create`.
7. Post.

## How to post as the group

1. Be an admin of the group.
2. In the composer, choose the group as the author.
3. The **group** becomes the owner of the post; **you** are recorded as
   provenance.

## How to read and react

1. Open the group's **Feed** tab.
2. The feed renders the newest **15** items and grows in steps of 15 via **Show
   more**. Nothing is hidden — rendering is just deferred so the page stays fast.
3. Click a post to open **`/posts/<id>`**.
4. **React** — toggle a like, or set a specific reaction type. Counts aggregate
   per user.
5. **Comment** — comments are threaded; a reply carries a `parentCommentId`. The
   same thread component powers job, project, and event discussions.
6. **Thank** — toggle a thank on a post.

## Good to know

- **Guests can read** public posts and post detail. Composing requires a
  session.
- **Home feed content is permission-filtered per actor** — private and
  group-scoped posts only reach eligible viewers.
- **Federation.** A post created here projects to peers as a signed event and
  materializes on the far side with its body and embeds intact. Cross-instance
  comments, likes, reactions, and RSVPs arrive over the shared mutation contract
  — see [Federation & SSO identity](/docs/wiki/federation-identity#the-shared-mutation-contract).
- **A post whose resource is sovereign-homed elsewhere redirects** to its source
  instance rather than rendering a hollow local mirror.
- Post pages emit structured data (JSON-LD) for link unfurling and search.
- Link previews are fetched server-side through `/api/link-preview`, which is
  SSRF-hardened.

## Under the hood

| Surface | Route | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Root → group page | `/` (307) | `src/middleware.ts`, `src/lib/home-tabs.ts`, fallback `src/app/(main)/page.tsx` |
| Group feed | `/groups/[id]?tab=feed` | `src/components/group-tabs-client.tsx` → `group-feed.tsx` |
| Post detail | `/posts/[id]` | `src/app/posts/[id]/page.tsx` → `post-detail-client.tsx` |
| Composer | `/create` (Post tab) | `src/app/(main)/create/page.tsx` → `src/components/create-post.tsx` |
| Design composer | `/create/design` | `src/app/(main)/create/design/page.tsx` |

- Post kinds: `PostType` in `src/lib/types.ts` — `social`, `offer`, `request`,
  `gratitude`.
- Creation: `createPostResource` in
  `src/app/actions/resource-creation/posts.ts`.
- Reactions: `src/app/actions/interactions/reactions.ts` —
  `toggleLikeOnTarget`, `setReactionOnTarget`, `toggleThankOnTarget`.
- Comments: `postCommentAction` in
  `src/app/actions/resource-creation/comments.ts`; UI
  `src/components/comment-feed.tsx`.
- Feed rendering: `src/components/post-feed.tsx` (windowed, +15 per Show more).
- Structured data: `src/lib/structured-data.ts`.
- Link previews: `src/app/api/link-preview/route.ts`.
- MCP: `rivr.posts.create`.
