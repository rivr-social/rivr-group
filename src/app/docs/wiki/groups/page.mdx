# Groups & subgroups

## What it is

Groups are agent-owned communities/orgs with their own feed, members, tabs, and
resources. Groups can nest (subgroups) with a lineage breadcrumb, and
org-descendant subgroups get the full org tab set. A group that is an
**affiliate/partner** of an org-grade group (a ledger `relationshipType` edge,
not a `parentId` subgroup edge) inherits that same org tab set — so partner-group
members see the Jobs board and other org tabs, not just subgroups. Rings and
families are related group-like containers.

## Where it lives

- `/groups` — directory · `src/app/(main)/groups/page.tsx`
- `/groups/[id]` — group home + tabs · `src/app/groups/[id]/page.tsx` (`group-tabs-client.tsx`)
- `/groups/[id]/settings` — group settings · `src/app/groups/[id]/settings/page.tsx`
- `/groups/[id]/docs` — group docs/filesystem · `src/app/groups/[id]/docs/page.tsx`
- `/rings`, `/rings/[id]`, `/families`, `/families/[id]` — related containers

## Every interaction

- **Join / leave** — toggle membership on a group or ring.
- **Request to join / approve** — request-gated groups; admins approve or deny.
- **Invite a member (admin)** — admins invite any person from the Members tab
  (search by name/username, choose the member or admin role). **Membership
  starts only when the person accepts** — the invite appears in their
  notifications and as an accept/decline banner on the group page; admins see
  and can cancel pending invitations. Also available to agents as the
  `rivr.groups.invite_member` MCP tool.
- **Browse tabs** — feed, members, events, projects, docs, and org tabs for
  org-typed groups and their descendant subgroups.
- **Lineage breadcrumb** — navigate up the subgroup → parent → org chain.
- **Post as the group** — admins author group-owned posts (see [Feeds & posts](/docs/wiki/feeds-posts)).
- **Org tab visibility** — org-grade tabs (jobs, marketplace, governance,
  badges, stake, treasury, docs, subgroups) default to **members** visibility:
  members see them, non-members and anonymous visitors don't. Admins can widen
  any tab to public or tighten it to admin-only/hidden per group from settings.
- **Manage settings** — name, description, visibility, membership policy, member
  capability toggles _(TODO(verify): enumerate the exact toggle set live)_.

## Membership dues & treasury

This group can charge **membership dues** (its paid plans, set in Settings →
Memberships). Members are billed through Stripe, grossed up so the group nets
the full plan price. Dues and other inflows land in the group **treasury**
(Treasury tab: funds, subgroup allocations, payouts, and — where enabled — a
**crypto USDC treasury** in an officer-controlled Safe). Federated members can
join and pay with their verified identity from their home instance.

## Permissions / gating

- **Public** group pages are readable by guests (`/groups` is a public prefix).
- **Members** see member-only content and can post/participate per capability.
- **Group admins** manage membership, settings, subgroups, and group authorship.
- Group-agent ownership: the group is itself an agent that can own resources.
