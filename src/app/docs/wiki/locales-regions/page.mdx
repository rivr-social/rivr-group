# Locales, regions, rings & families (global concepts)

## What it is

Place-scoped commons — locales/chapters, regions/bioregions (basins) — and the
ring/family affinity containers are **RIVR-ecosystem concepts hosted elsewhere**.
Locales and regions run as their own sovereign instances and aggregate on the
global hub. This group instance participates in that ecosystem; it does not
browse it.

**There is no `/locales`, `/basins`, `/rings`, or `/families` route here.**

## What survives on this instance

1. **Rings and families as types, not routes.** A group agent can be typed
   `ring` or `family`; it renders on the ordinary group page at
   `/groups/<id>` — never at `/rings/<id>` or `/families/<id>` (linking to
   those was a real 404 bug, fixed by the canonical-entity-link sweep).
2. **Their treasuries.** `ring-treasury-tab.tsx` and `family-treasury-tab.tsx`
   render the money surface for a ring- or family-typed container. Everything in
   [Wallet & treasury](/docs/wiki/wallet-treasury) applies.
3. **Location on the map, outward.** A group can publish a **map marker** so it
   appears on the global map — set it in group settings → **Map Marker**.

## Good to know

- Place *scoping* on posts and events (attaching a locale or region) is a global
  authoring affordance. Content created here reaches place feeds by federating
  outward, not by a local place picker.
- Location autocomplete in forms is served locally and sovereignly from this
  instance's own place/locale graph nodes (`/api/locations/suggest`) — it never
  calls out to global.

## Under the hood

- Ring/family treasuries: `src/components/ring-treasury-tab.tsx`,
  `src/components/family-treasury-tab.tsx`.
- Canonical link routing (why ring/family owners link to `/groups/<id>`):
  `src/lib/federation/entity-link.ts` + `src/components/canonical-link.tsx`.
- Map marker: the `map-marker` tab in `src/app/groups/[id]/settings/page.tsx`.
- Location suggestions: `src/app/api/locations/suggest/route.ts`.
