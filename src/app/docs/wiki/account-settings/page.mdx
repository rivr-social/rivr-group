# Account & settings

## What it is

Everything about **you** on this instance: your profile, who can see what, how
you're notified, your seller payment account, and the identities you link
outward. Plus — for the person who owns the instance — two operator pages.

## How to edit your account

1. Go to **`/settings`** (redirects to `/auth/login` if you aren't signed in).
2. You get six tabs:

| Tab | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Account** | name, username, email, bio, phone, avatar upload |
| **Privacy** | profile visibility, friend requests, transaction visibility, activity visibility, attribute visibility — grouped in accordions, saved with **Save Privacy Settings** |
| **Notifications** | push notifications, **email notifications**, event reminders, new messages, Murmurations publishing |
| **Appearance** | dark mode and display preferences |
| **Seller** | your Stripe USD wallet — set up or continue Connect onboarding |
| **Federation** | peer-node export permission, linked identities (PeerMesh Spatial, Bluesky / AT Protocol), manifest export preview, exported-event counts |

3. Save. Profile updates are **sparse** — only the fields you supply change, and
   an explicit `null` clears one.

![The settings page's six-tab strip — Account, Privacy, Notifications, Appearance, Seller, Federation — with the Account tab open showing name, username, email, and phone fields](/wiki/account-settings/group-06-settings-tabs.png)

## How to stop getting email

1. Go to **`/settings` → Notifications**.
2. Turn **Email Notifications** off.
3. Save.

**This is honored.** Group broadcasts (Settings → Announcements) and newsletters
both resolve member emails through a shared check and **exclude** anyone who has
opted out. Every outbound message also carries a non-tracking
unsubscribe/preferences footer pointing back at your notification settings — and
the footer is appended to the **outbound** copy only, so the stored body is never
polluted.

The check reads two shapes, so an older account is honored too:
`metadata.emailNotifications` (top level) and
`metadata.notificationSettings.emailNotifications` (nested).

## How to update your profile page

1. Go to **`/profile`**. Your profile has eleven tabs: **About**, **Posts**,
   **Events**, **Groups**, **Photos**, **Offerings**, **Calendar**, **Wallet**,
   **Personas**, **Saved**, **Activity**.
2. Your public profile is at `/profile/<username>`.
3. Wallet and money live under the **Wallet** tab — see
   [Wallet & treasury](/docs/wiki/wallet-treasury).
4. Personas live under the **Personas** tab — see
   [Assistant, personas & MCP](/docs/wiki/autobot-agents).

## How to sign in, sign up, or reset a password

Public auth pages:

- **`/auth/login`** and **`/login`**
- **`/auth/signup`** — a stepped flow: login method → name → email → phone →
  birthday → terms → verify → save login → confirm
- **`/auth/forgot-password`** and **`/auth/reset-password`**

Federated members sign in through their home instance — see
[Federation & SSO identity](/docs/wiki/federation-identity).

## Owner-only pages

These are not part of `/settings`; navigate to them directly.

- **`/settings/outgoing-email`** — this instance's transactional SMTP
  credentials, a test-send, and a delete-and-fall-back-to-relay action.
  Instance admins only. Federated-auth email routing is deliberately **not**
  exposed — it is pinned by the mailer so a peer admin cannot redirect
  authentication mail.
- **`/settings/visitor-access`** — the policy for federated visitors landing here
  from another instance: which capabilities they're granted and how long their
  session lasts, plus a log of recent landings (referral path, home instance,
  granted scope). **Instance owner only**; also barred at the middleware for
  non-owner federated visitors.

![The visitor-access page: capability checkboxes (react, comment, RSVP, message), the session-length select, the Record visits toggle, and the top of the Recent visitor landings log](/wiki/account-settings/group-07-visitor-access.png)

## Good to know

- **Every settings surface requires a session.** Billing and seller actions
  derive the subject from the session — never from client input.
- **Social links are validated server-side** in `updateProfileAction`, not just
  in the browser.
- **There is no account data-export or account-deletion UI in this build.**
  Earlier docs described a full JSON export (`rivr.account.export_data`) and a
  typed-confirmation deletion flow; neither exists on this instance. The
  Federation tab's "Manifest Export Preview" is a *federation identity*
  manifest, not an account data export.
- The instance-owner pages redirect non-owners, but the redirect is a
  convenience — each API route re-checks authority.

- Profile actions: `src/app/actions/settings.ts` — `updateProfileAction`,
  `updateProfileImageAction`, `updateGroupImageAction`; validation
  `src/lib/social-links.ts`.

  `resolveGroupMemberEmails` and `sendNewsletterAction`
  (`src/app/actions/newsletter.ts`). Tests:
  `src/app/actions/__tests__/newsletter-optout.test.ts`.

  (`VISITOR_CAPABILITIES`, `BASELINE_VISITOR_CAPABILITY`,
  `DEFAULT_VISITOR_TTL_MINUTES`).
- Admin APIs: `/api/admin/smtp-config` (+ `/test`), `/api/admin/visitor-access`.
