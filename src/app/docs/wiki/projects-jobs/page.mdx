# Projects & jobs

## What it is

The work-graph surfaces. Projects are coordination containers (with treasury,
deliverables, and lead/QA roles); jobs/tasks are claimable units of work, often
gated on badges. Together they model who is doing what and how contribution is
rewarded — work is **claimed finished** by the worker and **attested** by the
project's QA before stake moves.

## Where it lives

- `/projects` — project directory · `src/app/(main)/projects/page.tsx`
- `/projects/[id]` — project detail · `src/app/(main)/projects/[id]/page.tsx`
- `/jobs` — job board · `src/app/jobs/page.tsx`
- `/jobs/[id]` — job detail · `src/app/jobs/[id]/page.tsx` (`JobDetailClient`)

## Every interaction

- **Browse / open** a project or job.
- **Create / update a project** — name, description, treasury, deliverables, and
  a timeframe set through the unified date-time range selector (start/end in one
  popover) on both the create form and the owner edit dialog.
- **Set project roles** — a **Lead** (coordinator, default attester) and a
  **QA** on the project's Team tab. QA defaults to the lead; it can be any
  member of the group, its parent, a sibling, or a child subgroup — or an
  **affiliated group agent**, whose admins then hold the QA authority.
- **Claim a job** — take a job. Claiming always requires **membership in the
  owning group** (group/parent-group admins also qualify and bypass badge
  gates); jobs may additionally be badge-gated, admin-gated, slot-capped, or
  approval-gated.
- **Check off a task ("claim finished")** — the worker's check-off records a
  claim and lands the task in *awaiting attestation*; if a work timer is
  running, the task links to that work period automatically. Workers can
  propose a point value with their claim.
- **Attest a task** — the QA, lead, or a group admin attests (or rejects) the
  claim from the point chip on the task; **stake points land at attestation**
  (the attester can adjust the awarded value). A verifier's own check-off
  attests in one click.
- **Claim a job complete** — an assignee claims the whole job finished, rating
  the work's **skillfulness required** and **difficulty** (1–100 sliders);
  the reviewing admin settles it by marking the job done.
- **Split job points (Points tab)** — task-less jobs carry a job-level point
  pool. Each assignee sets sliders over the *other* assignees; the pool splits
  by the average of everyone's ratings (equal split until anyone rates) when
  the job is marked done.
- **Add tasks to a claimed job** — active claimants (not just admins) can add
  tasks to their claimed job; their proposed points still settle through
  attestation.
- **Time bounds** — jobs and tasks carry a work window (start → deadline) and
  a **max-hours budget**; hourly pay never exceeds the job's budget (overage
  stays visible on the timesheet but doesn't pay).
- **Job payouts draw from the project's treasury** — when a cash job belongs
  to a project, marking it done pays assignees from that **project's wallet**
  (the budget the group approved into it). An underfunded project parks the
  payout as *pending funds* — top up the project wallet and re-run mark-done;
  it never silently dips into the group treasury. Jobs outside any project
  pay from the owning group's treasury as before.
- **Volunteer pay (Thanks voucher)** — a job's fourth pay method, alongside
  fixed cash, hourly cash, and points. **No cash moves.** When a volunteer
  job is marked done, each volunteer receives — post-hoc — a **Thanks voucher**
  the group *claims from them*: valued from the **skillfulness × difficulty**
  ratings they set when claiming the job complete (× hours worked, the same
  formula the voucher builder uses), it is redeemed by the group and mints that
  many **Thanks tokens** to the volunteer. Idempotent — re-running mark-done
  mints nothing twice.
- **Complete a project (settles the treasury)** — a group admin or the
  project lead completes the project; expenses are already first-class
  debits, so the remaining project-wallet balance — the **net** — sweeps
  into the **owning group's treasury** automatically (idempotent; run an
  explicit net distribution first when the net should split beyond the
  owning group). Every subgroup has a treasury to receive it. Also available
  as the `rivr.projects.complete` and `rivr.treasury.ensure_subgroup_wallets`
  MCP tools.
- **Update job status** — progress a job through its lifecycle.
- **Link to project** — jobs and events can link to a parent project.
- **Calendar** — your completed work periods and claimed jobs' windows appear
  on `/calendar` (filterable); group admins additionally see a per-member
  "Work periods" kind on the group calendar.

## Stock (Inventory + Needs)

Orgs, projects, and jobs each have a **Stock** tab with two subtabs:

- **Inventory** — the material resources (REA `resource`/`asset` items) associated
  with that object (an org's owned assets, a project's `projectId`-linked stock, a
  job's `jobId`-linked stock).
- **Needs** — a simple editable shopping list on the object (name, quantity, note,
  fulfilled). Each item can be **posted as a request** (a `Request` post) so the
  community can help fill it. Editing needs and posting requests is limited to
  the object's owner/admins.

## Permissions / gating

- **Guests** can view public projects/jobs.
- **Claiming requires group membership** (or group/ancestor admin authority);
  subscriptions gate group and subgroup *memberships*, never jobs.
- Job claims can additionally be **badge-gated** (admins bypass), admin-gated,
  slot-capped, or approval-gated.
- **Attestation** (task completed/rejected) is QA / lead / group-admin scoped.
- Project/treasury management is owner/admin-scoped; group-calendar work
  periods are admin-only.
