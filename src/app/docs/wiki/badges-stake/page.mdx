# Badges & stake

## What it is

Badges are issued credentials that recognize skills, roles, or contribution and
can gate actions (e.g. job claims). Points-based stake converts contribution into
standing/weight within an org. Admins manage badge issuance.

## Where it lives

- `/badges`, `/badges/[id]` — badge directory + detail · `src/app/badges/**`
- `/admin/badges` — badge administration · `src/app/admin/badges/page.tsx`
- `/points` — points/stake · `src/app/(main)/points/page.tsx`

## Every interaction

- **Browse badges / open a badge** — see a badge and its holders.
- **Issue a badge** — admins award badges to agents (`/admin/badges`).
- **Earn points (claim → attest)** — checking off a task records the worker's
  *claimed finished* event; points land as `earn` ledger rows **when the
  project's QA, lead, or a group admin attests** the work (the attester can
  adjust the value). Reopening or rejecting work retracts its points. Task-less
  jobs carry a job-level pool split among assignees by their mutual
  peer-assessment sliders at job completion.
- **Stake** — org stake is proportional to attested task points earned across
  the group and its subgroup tree: `profitShare = your points ÷ subtree total`,
  shown on the group's Stake tab and reused by net-allocation class rules.
  Badges remain claim-time *gates*; completion records contribution instead of
  minting badges.

## Permissions / gating

- **Public** badge pages (`/badges` is a public prefix).
- **Badge issuance** is admin-gated (`/admin/badges`).
- Badge holdings gate downstream capabilities (e.g. claiming badge-gated jobs).
