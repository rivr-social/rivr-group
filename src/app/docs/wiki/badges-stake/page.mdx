# Badges & stake

## What it is

Two different ways the group recognizes people.

A **badge** is an issued credential — a skill, a role, a qualification. Badges
are mostly **gates**: a job can require one before it can be claimed. Badges are
awarded by group admins; they are not minted by finishing work.

**Stake** is a share of standing derived from **attested task points**. Your
stake in an org is your points divided by the total points earned across that
org and its whole subgroup tree. It is what net-distribution class rules pay by.

<Callout type="note" title="There is no /points page and no /admin/badges">
  Points and stake live on the group's <strong>Stake</strong> tab. Badges are
  browsed at <code>/badges</code> and issued from the group's
  <strong>Badges</strong> tab. Earlier docs cited <code>/points</code> and
  <code>/admin/badges</code>; neither route exists here.
</Callout>

## How to browse badges

1. Go to **`/badges`** — the badge directory. It shows every badge definition
   and marks which ones you hold.
2. Click a badge to open **`/badges/<id>`**: the badge, its holders, and its
   **Available Jobs** — the jobs this badge unlocks, each with its pay badge.

## How to create and award a badge (admin)

1. Open the group's **Badges** tab (`/groups/<id>?tab=badges`).
2. Click **Create Badge** and fill in the definition.
3. On any badge card, use the **Award to member…** picker to choose a member,
   then click **Award**.

## How to earn points and stake

1. Claim a job and do the work — see [Projects & jobs](/docs/wiki/projects-jobs).
2. **Check off a task.** This records a *claim*, not points. You may propose a
   point value.
3. The project's **QA**, its **lead**, or a **group-or-ancestor admin** attests
   the claim. **Points land at attestation**, and the attester may adjust the
   awarded value.
4. Rejecting or reopening the work deactivates both the claim and its points.
5. Task-less jobs carry a job-level point pool that splits by peer assessment at
   mark-done.

## How to read the Stake tab

1. Open the group's **Stake** tab (`/groups/<id>?tab=stake`).
2. **Your points by subgroup** — a card showing how your points are split across
   the org's subgroups. It's a read-only partition by the task's direct owner, so
   the parts sum exactly to your subtree total.
3. **Stake Overview** — the group stake distribution: each member with their
   `profitShare` percentage and a bar.
4. **Contribution Metrics** — recorded contributions per member.

## Good to know

- **The maths.** `profitShare = your points ÷ subtree total points`, over the
  recursive subgroup tree. Net-allocation `class` rules split their basis points
  proportionally to the same weights, falling back to an equal split only when
  nobody in the class holds points.
- **Badges gate; they don't reward.** Completion records contribution; it does
  not mint a badge. Job claims can be badge-gated, and **group and ancestor
  admins bypass badge gates**.
- **Badges also feed governance.** A poll or proposal can restrict voting to
  badge holders (any badge, or one specific governance badge), and a governance
  badge can carry a **voting weight**. See [Governance](/docs/wiki/governance).
- **Share classes are separate.** Steward/Worker equity — a percentage of org net
  split among members by shares held — lives on the **Treasury** tab, not here,
  and is authored per share class. It also supplies the `vote %` governance
  dimension.
- Badge pages are public; the group's Badges and Stake tabs are members-default.
- There is exactly **one writer** for the points ledger edge, and it only fires
  at attestation.

- **Points writer:** `src/lib/work-completion.ts` (`attestWork`) — writes the

  re-attest updates in place; reject/reopen deactivates.

  `getMemberStakesForGroup`, `getMemberSubgroupPointsBreakdown`,
  `getSubtreeTaskPointsByMember`.

  `splitBpsByWeight`, folded with `resolveShareClassAllocations`.
- **Share classes:** `src/app/actions/wallet/share-classes.ts`.
- **Job badge gates:** `src/components/job-required-badges-field.tsx`.
