# Marketplace, listings & fees

## What it is

The place this group buys and sells. A **listing** is an offering — a service,
product, skill, voucher, resource, ticket, bounty, trip, or gift — posted by a
member or by the group itself. Buyers pay by card (Stripe), by internal Rivr
wallet balance, or in USDC on Base. Bookable listings hand the buyer a time slot
before checkout. Membership dues are sold from separate product pages.

The seller **always nets the price they typed.** Every fee is added on top and
paid by the buyer.

## How to list something

1. Open the group's **Marketplace** tab (`/groups/<id>?tab=marketplace`) and
   click **Add Listing** — or open a listing you own and edit it. You must be a
   group member.
2. Choose the **offering type**: service, product, skill, resource, voucher,
   venue, ticket, bounty, trip, data, or gift. The form changes to match —
   services ask for an hourly rate, products for a unit price and condition,
   bounties for a reward and criteria, and so on.
3. Fill in name, description, images, category, and price. `service` and
   `product` are priced by default; `gift` is free by default.
4. Pick the **currency** and any additional accepted currencies.
5. If the listing should be **bookable**, scroll to **Bookable Schedule** and add
   week-view blocks you can be booked for. Types that support it: service,
   resource, voucher, product, skill.
6. Choose who the offering is targeted at and whether to post it to the group's
   feed.
7. Save. The listing appears in the group's Marketplace tab and at
   `/marketplace`.

## How to buy

1. Open `/marketplace`, or the group's Marketplace tab, and click a listing to
   reach **`/marketplace/<id>`**.
2. Click through to **`/marketplace/<id>/purchase`**.
3. If the listing is bookable, **pick a date and a time slot**. Checkout stays
   disabled until a valid slot is selected; a listing with no declared blocks
   skips the picker entirely.
4. Set quantity / hours if the type asks for them.
5. Choose a payment method — **card**, your **Rivr wallet** balance, or **crypto
   (USDC)** where the listing and instance support it. The page preselects the
   best available option.
6. Check the breakdown: item price, **Platform fee**, **Total**.
7. Pay. On success you land on **`/marketplace/<id>/confirmed`**, and your
   receipt is at **`/marketplace/<id>/receipt/<receiptId>`**.

8. Your purchase history lives at **`/profile/purchases`** and in
   **`/profile?tab=wallet&walletTab=purchases`**.

## The fee model (one model, fleet-wide)

Since 2026-07-20 there is exactly **one** RIVR margin across every transaction
type — marketplace, offerings, tickets, dues, crypto. The old scattered models
(a flat 5%, a 4% + 40¢ "payment leg", a baked-in 9.05% "sales tax") are retired.

Starting from the **seller's price**, the buyer total is built as:

| Component | Amount | Constant |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RIVR margin, percent | **3.3%** of seller price | `MARKETPLACE_FEE_BPS = 330` |
| RIVR margin, flat | **$1.49** | `PLATFORM_MARGIN_FIXED_CENTS = 149` |
| Connect-account overhead | **5% of the cart, capped at $2.00** | `CONNECT_OVERHEAD_RECOVERY_BPS = 500`, cap `200¢` |
| Org commission | 0 unless the owning org sets `commissionBps` | — |
| Referral splits | 0 unless configured server-side | — |
| Cross-border surcharge | see below | — |
| **Then** the whole thing is grossed up | so that after Stripe's **2.9% + 30¢** the platform still nets the above | `STRIPE_CARD_PERCENT_BPS = 290`, `STRIPE_CARD_FIXED_CENTS = 30` |

Consequences worth knowing:

- The **seller receives the listed price**, exactly. `sellerNetCents === sellerPriceCents`.
- The Connect overhead used to be a flat $2 on every purchase, which made a $6
  item carry a ~$2.86 buyer fee (~48%). It now scales: a $6 cart recovers 30¢,
  and **every cart ≥ $40 prices exactly as before**.
- Membership **dues** run the same gross-up but with **zero flat Connect
  overhead** — dues carry no per-purchase account cost. See
  [Wallet & treasury](/docs/wiki/wallet-treasury#membership-dues-pricing).

## Cross-border corridor pricing

When the seller's payout account is not in the platform's country, Stripe
charges extra to get the money there. RIVR prices that **into the buyer total**
so it never comes out of the seller's face value or RIVR's margin. Three
corridors are modelled (`SellerPayoutCorridor`):

| Corridor | Who | Buyer-funded surcharge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `domestic` | seller in the platform country | none |
| `connect_cross_border` | seller in another Stripe **Connect region** | **0.25% + 25¢** (`CROSS_BORDER_TRANSFER_BPS = 25`, `CROSS_BORDER_PAYOUT_FLAT_CENTS = 25`) |
| `global_payouts` | seller outside the Connect regions (e.g. Costa Rica) | **$1.50 + 1.25% volume + 1% FX** (`GLOBAL_PAYOUT_FLAT_CENTS = 150`, `GLOBAL_PAYOUT_VOLUME_BPS = 125`, `GLOBAL_PAYOUT_FX_BPS = 100`) |

<Callout type="warning" title="Scoping — corridors are resolved on GLOBAL, not here">
  <p>
    <strong>GLOBAL holds every Connect account and makes every real payout.</strong>
    The seller's country picker and the payout-setup flow live in the global repo.
    This instance ships the shared corridor-aware fee engine so pricing is
    consistent fleet-wide, but its own checkout call sites currently pass no
    corridor — they compute at the <code>domestic</code> default. Do not expect a
    country picker on this instance; there isn't one.
  </p>
  <p>
    The <code>global_payouts</code> constants are <strong>provisional</strong> per
    Stripe's pricing trail and are to be re-confirmed when that integration lands.
  </p>
</Callout>

## Sales tax (Stripe Tax)

RIVR is a Colorado Public Benefit LCA acting as a **marketplace facilitator**.
Sales tax is **destination-based**: Stripe Tax computes it from the *buyer's*
address at checkout. This replaced a flat 9.05% Boulder rate that was previously
charged to every buyer and kept as revenue.

- **Tax-exclusive.** The listed amount is what the seller receives; tax is added
  **on top**, never carved out of the seller's net (`RIVR_TAX_BEHAVIOR = 'exclusive'`).
- **Product tax codes.** Default is **General – Services** (`txcd_20030000`).
  A listing explicitly typed `product` that isn't a ticket in product clothing
  gets **General – Tangible Goods** (`txcd_99999999`). Offerings, services,
  vouchers, tickets, and legacy listings stay on services — the conservative
  failure mode is under-collection, never a wrong charge.
- **Inert until registered.** `automatic_tax` collects tax **only** in
  jurisdictions where RIVR has an active registration in the Stripe dashboard
  (Settings → Tax → Registrations). Until those exist, **nobody is charged tax.**
- **Checkout-Session paths only.** Marketplace checkout, tickets, and group/org
  dues run through Checkout Sessions and carry the tax config. The
  destination-charge paths (offerings and bookings, which use raw
  PaymentIntents) need the Tax Calculation API instead — not yet wired.
- On the Connect rail the charge routes to a connected account, so RIVR-the-
  facilitator is set as the party **liable** to collect and remit
  (`liability.type = 'self'`).

## Paying with crypto (USDC)

Listings priced in USDC can be paid straight from a browser wallet on **Base**.
Where the settlement splitter is enabled, it is **one signature**: you approve a
single payment authorization (no gas, nothing to submit) and the platform
settles the seller's share and the platform fee atomically in one on-chain
transaction — neither leg can happen without the other. The network is
operator-configured per instance; the current posture is **Base Sepolia
(testnet)**, matching the Stripe test-keys posture.

## Membership products

`/products/membership-basic`, `-host`, `-organizer`, `-seller`, `-steward` are
marketing + checkout pages for the cooperative membership tiers, each with a
monthly/yearly toggle. `/subscribe` is different — on this sovereign instance it
sells **this group's own** dues plans, never the cooperative tiers.

## Good to know

- **Buyer identity is derived server-side** from the session (or a federated
  remote-viewer cookie) — never from client input. A signed-in federated member
  is recognized; they used to be wrongly shown the "sign in to complete your
  purchase" modal.
- **Federated mirror listings redirect.** Checkout for a listing that is
  sovereign-homed elsewhere is bounced to the source instance, so price,
  inventory, fee config, and Stripe settlement all run where the listing lives.
- Order numbers on the confirmation page read `ORD-XXXXXXXX`: the last 8
  alphanumerics of the per-purchase order/session id, uppercased.
- Marketplace products and event tickets settle through the **platform
  capital-accounts** model (internal ledger credit to the group plus a separate
  Connect payout rail), not a destination charge. Offerings and group
  subscriptions *do* use real destination charges.
- Listing browse is public; buying requires a session.
