# Marketplace & gifts

## What it is

The marketplace lets agents list offerings and buyers purchase them via Stripe,
with receipts and confirmation flows. Alongside paid exchange, RIVR supports
gift/mutual-aid economy through thanks-tokens. Membership products are also sold
here.

## Where it lives

- `/marketplace`, `/marketplace/[id]` — listings · `src/app/(main)/marketplace/**`
- `/marketplace/[id]/purchase`, `/confirmed`, `/receipt/[receiptId]` — checkout flow
- `/products/membership-*` — membership products · `src/app/products/**`
- `/profile/purchases` — purchase history · `src/app/(main)/profile/purchases/page.tsx`

## Every interaction

- **Browse / open a listing.**
- **Save a listing** — add to saved listings.
- **Purchase** — Stripe checkout. The buyer identity is derived **server-side**
  from the session (never from client input); on success a receipt resource is
  created and shown at `/marketplace/[id]/confirmed` → `/receipt/[receiptId]`.
- **Create an offering/listing** — sellers (with the seller membership) create
  listings.
- **Send thanks tokens** — gift one or more thanks tokens to another agent,
  optionally attaching a message or the resource being thanked.

## Paying with crypto (USDC)

Listings priced in USDC can be paid straight from a browser wallet on the Base
network. Where this instance has the settlement splitter enabled, it is **one
signature**: you approve a single payment authorization (no gas, no
transaction to submit) and the platform settles the seller's share and the
platform fee atomically in one on-chain transaction — neither leg can happen
without the other. The instance's network is operator-configured; the current
posture is **Base Sepolia (testnet)**, matching the Stripe test-keys posture.

## Permissions / gating

- **Public** listing browse (`/marketplace`, `/products` are public prefixes).
- **Buying** requires a session; **selling** typically requires the seller
  membership tier.
- Checkout buyer metadata is set server-side (security-hardened, issue #101).

## Booking & availability

Bookable offerings (services, skills, vouchers, products, resources) declare a
**Bookable Schedule** at creation — week-view blocks the seller can be booked
for ("Add booking times" in the composer). Buyers then pick one block in the
purchase flow before checkout; the chosen slot is consumed on booking. A
listing with no declared blocks stays non-bookable and checkout skips the
picker.

## Fees on small purchases

The buyer-paid gross-up covers Stripe processing, the platform margin, and a
Connect-account overhead that now SCALES with the cart (capped at $2), so
micro-purchases no longer carry a ~50% fee; carts ≥ $40 price exactly as
before. The seller always nets the listed price.
