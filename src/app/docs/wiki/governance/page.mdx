# Governance

## What it is

Group-level decision-making: proposals, votes/decisions, and the rules by which a
group or org coordinates authority. Governance is tied to membership, badges, and
points-based stake.

## Where it lives

- The **Voice** tab within the group page — `src/components/governance-tab.tsx`,
  mounted from `group-tabs-client.tsx`. It has two sub-tabs: **Issues & Polls**
  and **Proposals & Votes**.
- Items persist on the group agent's `metadata` (`proposals`, `polls`, `issues`
  arrays) and are read back through `group-tabs-client` into the tab.
- Related: stake and badges (`/badges`, `/points`)

## Every interaction

Three creatable governance item types, each with its own composer at the top of
the Voice tab:

- **Create a proposal** — a yes / no / abstain decision with a pass **threshold**
  (%) and a **duration** (days). `createGovernanceProposalAction`.
- **Create a poll** — a lightweight multi-option question (2+ options) with a
  **duration** and a **ballot style**: multiple choice, approval, score,
  ranked (instant-runoff), rate + rank, or quadratic (per-voter credit budget).
  `createGovernancePollAction`. Distinct from a proposal: a poll gathers
  preference across several choices rather than ratifying a single motion.
- **Create an issue** — a titled discussion item (title + description).
  `createGovernanceIssueAction`.
- **Vote / decide** — the Vote button opens the voting modal. Proposals take
  yes/no/abstain; polls take one of their options. `castGovernanceVoteAction`
  records the vote (one active vote per member per item; re-voting supersedes the
  prior vote).
- **View items / results** — proposals show yes/no tallies + threshold; polls
  show per-option percentages.

## Permissions / gating (eligibility gates)

- **Creating** a proposal, poll, or issue requires **group write access** by
  default (`hasGroupWriteAccess` — group/subgroup admins, cascading to
  parent-group admins). Admins can **widen** propose authority from the
  Voice tab's Governance Settings (gear icon) — e.g. to all members or badge
  holders — via `setGovernanceProposeGateAction`; admins always retain it.
  Server-enforced in each create action; the client cannot bypass it.
- **Voting** is gated per item by its **eligibility gate**, chosen at creation
  ("Who can vote"): **group members** (the default), **anyone signed in**,
  **badge holders** (any or a specific governance badge), **admins only**,
  **share holders** (any or a specific share class, with a minimum share
  count), or **custom policy** (the group's permission policies for the
  `vote` verb). The gate defines the electorate exactly — admins do not
  bypass it. Each item's card shows its gate as a chip, and the Vote button
  is disabled (with the reason) for ineligible viewers; the server re-enforces
  in `castGovernanceVoteAction`, which is also rate-limited (one active vote
  per voter per item; re-voting supersedes the prior vote).
