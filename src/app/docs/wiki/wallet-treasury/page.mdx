# Wallet & treasury

## What it is

Two different pots of money.

**Your wallet** is personal: an internal Rivr balance (a platform IOU — instant
and fee-free between Rivr accounts), your purchase and sales history, and — if
you sell — your Stripe Connect balance and the controls to turn Rivr credit into
real dollars in your bank.

**The group treasury** is collective: the group's balance, named funds, project
wallets, subgroup wallets, budgets, and reports. Money enters from dues, sales,
and grants; it flows out to projects, subgroups, and worker payouts.

<Callout type="warning" title="There is no /wallet or /points page">
  Your wallet lives at <code>/profile?tab=wallet</code>. The group treasury lives
  on the group page's <strong>Treasury</strong> tab. Earlier docs listed
  <code>/wallet</code> and <code>/points</code> routes — they do not exist in this
  codebase.
</Callout>

## How to check your wallet

1. Go to **`/profile`** and open the **Wallet** tab
   (`/profile?tab=wallet`).
2. Pick a sub-tab — the URL tracks it as `&walletTab=…`:
   - **Transactions** (`walletTab=transactions`) — your internal Rivr balance and
     its ledger.
   - **Purchases** (`walletTab=purchases`) — what you have bought.
   - **Sales** (`walletTab=sales`) — what you have sold, plus the seller
     payment controls below.

## How to get paid out (seller cash-out)

Everything here is on the **Sales** sub-tab.

1. Go to **`/profile?tab=wallet&walletTab=sales`**.
2. If you have no payment account yet, click **Set up payments** — you are sent
   to Stripe onboarding and returned to this same sub-tab. If onboarding is
   partial, the card shows **Charges / Payouts / Details** badges and a
   **Complete Setup** button.
3. Once onboarded you see your **Connect balance** (available and pending), your
   Rivr wallet balance, and — where enabled — your treasury and linked-bank
   balances. **Link bank** appears when Financial Connections is available.
4. To convert internal Rivr credit into real money: type an amount in the
   **Move Rivr wallet balance to Stripe** field and click **Move to Stripe**.
5. To send real money to your bank, click **Bank payout (1-3 days)** or
   **Instant payout**. These only appear when your Connect available balance is
   above zero.
6. **Seller Dashboard** opens your Stripe express/custom dashboard.

### What "Move to Stripe" actually does

Your Rivr wallet is a platform IOU. Converting it means real dollars move.

- The action runs as a **saga**: debit the wallet and write a `pending` ledger
  row in one locked transaction → request the federated transfer, keyed on that
  row's id so retries are safe → mark it `completed`.
- Anything other than a `paid` result **refunds the wallet** and marks the row
  `failed`. Money is never lost and never minted.
- On this sovereign instance the real transfer is fulfilled by **GLOBAL**, the
  single Connect authority — it resolves your globally-held connected account
  and performs the Stripe Transfer there.
- In Stripe test mode a **Release Test Sales To Wallet** button also appears.

## How the group gets money to a worker ("Release payment")

Paying for finished work is deliberately two steps, so nobody's card is charged
by an accident of workflow.

1. An admin marks the job **done** on `/jobs/<id>`. That records the internal
   obligation: a contribution edge per assignee and a `transferP2P` from the
   **project's wallet** (if the job belongs to a project) or the group
   settlement wallet. The payout receipt is stamped **`awaiting_attestation`**.
2. The admin then clicks **Release payment** on the job admin panel. This attests
   the payout and asks GLOBAL to make the real Stripe transfer.
3. The button is **idempotent and re-runnable** — re-clicking retries only the
   payees that failed. Once every payout receipt has settled, the button becomes
   a **Paid ✓** indicator.

Each attempt records one of six verdicts on the receipt:

| Verdict | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `awaiting_attestation` | job marked done; an admin still has to release |
| `paid` | global settled a real Stripe transfer to the payee's connected account |
| `disabled` | `STRIPE_CONNECT_PAYOUTS_ENABLED` is off |
| `needs_onboarding` | payee has a global connected account that can't receive transfers yet |
| `insufficient_funds` | global's platform balance can't cover the transfer |
| `error` | global rejected the request or was unreachable (message captured) |

**How the two instances talk:** this instance POSTs to global's
`/api/federation/connect/payout` with peer auth (`x-peer-slug` +
`x-peer-secret`, falling back to `x-node-admin-key`), 15-second timeout,
best-effort — it never throws, so a slow global can never break a job payout.
**GLOBAL holds every connected account; this instance holds none.**

## How to use the group treasury

1. Open the group page and choose the **Treasury** tab
   (`/groups/<id>?tab=treasury`). It is **members**-visible by default.
2. The header cards always show **Current Balance**, **Monthly Revenue**,
   **Monthly Expenses**, and **Net Income (this month)**.
3. Then pick one of the **five sub-tabs**:

| Sub-tab | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | revenue streams and expense breakdown for the group |
| **Allocations** | the flow chart of how money moves between entities (see below) |
| **Transactions** | the consolidated treasury ledger — recent activity and the full list |
| **Budget** | committed vs. spent vs. authored budget, rolled up project → subgroup → group |
| **Reports** | financial reports (This month / Last month / YTD / All-time) with CSV + JSON export |

### Reading the Allocations chart

It draws how funds flow among entities: project wallets through expenses into
the group treasury (completion sweeps), treasury into funds and member payouts,
subgroup treasuries cascading upward, and the configured net-allocation splits.
**Solid arrows are realized transfers; dashed arrows are configured or pending
flows.**

### The consolidated ledger

The Transactions sub-tab resolves the group's **whole treasury tree** — main
settlement wallet, named funds, project wallets, and subgroup wallets (recursive
`parent_id`) — and classifies every leg as external inflow, external outflow, or
internal move, attributing it to the wallet that moved it. Internal moves are
net-zero, so funding a project never double-counts as revenue.

- **Treasury managers** see the full tree. **Members** see only the group
  settlement wallet's legs.
- This replaced an older version that read the *viewer's personal* wallet and
  therefore never showed job payouts (which debit the project wallet).

## Moving money around inside the group

- **Group → subgroup:** the Subgroup banking card lists each subgroup with a
  **Fund** control. This is an internal transfer and works with **no Stripe
  configuration at all**; it is gated on parent-group manage authority and a
  child-of-parent check.
- **Group/subgroup → project:** the project page's Treasury tab has a **Move
  money** control for treasury managers — apportion treasury into the project
  wallet to give it a spendable budget (expenses require a funded wallet), or
  return unspent balance. **Completed projects refuse new funding** — their
  residue belongs to the completion sweep. Project wallets also fill from sales
  of project-bound offerings.
- **Project → worker:** mark a job done, then Release payment (above).
- **Named funds:** create sub-pools of the main treasury ("Operations Fund",
  "Land Fund"), assign each subgroup to at most one fund, and move money
  main ↔ fund. Each move is capped by `MAX_TRANSFER_CENTS`.

## Membership dues pricing

This group's paid membership plans are priced with a **gross-up**: the member's
recurring charge is the plan price plus RIVR's margin plus card-processing cost,
so **the group nets the full plan price it set**. The buyer sees the total at
Stripe Checkout before paying.

- Dues run with **zero flat Connect overhead** — that stays a marketplace-only
  policy. They still carry the gross-up over Stripe's 2.9% + 30¢.
- On the Connect rail the `application_fee_percent` is derived from the gross-up
  (fee ÷ buyer total), so after Stripe's cut the group still nets face value.
  A flat percentage of face value used to net RIVR **negative** on small dues.
- An admin-configured Stripe price id is honored **only** when it already covers
  the grossed-up total, matching currency and interval; otherwise the code falls
  back to dynamic pricing and logs why.
- Federated members — people homed on another RIVR instance — can pay dues,
  subscribe, deposit, and buy here with their verified identity.

## Crypto treasury (USDC on Base)

The group can hold a digital-dollar treasury in a **Safe multisig its officers
control** — the platform never holds a key.

1. From the Treasury tab, an admin binds or deploys the officer Safe (2-of-N).
2. Officers approve treasury actions by signing **in the app** with their own
   wallets; the platform submits execution, so officers need no gas.
3. Officers approve a per-lead spending **budget** once; the lead then pays
   recipients within it with a single signature — no per-payment officer
   sign-off, remaining budget enforced on-chain.
4. USDC marketplace sales settle to the treasury atomically.

Current posture: **Base Sepolia (testnet)**.

## Banking (built, dormant behind flags)

Group admins will find a **Banking** area in the Treasury tab once the
platform's Stripe Treasury/Issuing programs are approved. Until
`STRIPE_TREASURY_ENABLED` / `STRIPE_ISSUING_ENABLED` are on, the card explains
the state instead of showing dead controls. What is built:

- **Group payment account** — the group holds its own Stripe Connect account.
- **Per-subgroup financial accounts** — each subgroup treasury can get its own
  Stripe Treasury financial account, hosted on the parent group's payment
  account, with its own balance.
- **Per-project and per-fund financial accounts** — the same lane for projects
  and named funds.
- **Spending cards** — a spending-limited virtual card per subgroup / project /
  fund, funded only by that entity's financial account (default cap
  **$500/month**; admins set the limit).
- **Connected bank balances** — link a real-world bank account (Financial
  Connections) and see the live external balance alongside the Stripe balances.

## Good to know

- **Points are not money.** Stake points come from attested task work and are
  shown on the group's **Stake** tab, not in the wallet. See
  [Badges & stake](/docs/wiki/badges-stake).
- **Thanks tokens** are a gift/mutual-aid instrument, transferred (never minted)
  and subject to demurrage. Volunteer job payouts redeem Thanks the group
  actually holds. See [Projects & jobs](/docs/wiki/projects-jobs).
- All wallet, subscription, and treasury surfaces require a session. Billing
  actions derive the subject from the session — never from client input.
- Admin surfaces gate on **server-computed** authority passed as props; the
  client user-context cannot see federated remote-viewer sessions.
